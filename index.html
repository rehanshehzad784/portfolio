Zinggo

Food ordering

novaCTO · Technical Lead · Play Store · 2024 — now

For a customer who wants food, and for the shop that prepares it.

The customer picks items and checks out. The shop sees that order on a merchant screen, updates stock, and the customer can see the status change. A push notification fires when the order moves.

My part

I lead the Flutter work with backend, QA, and product. Checkout, the merchant POS, live status, and the Play Store release sit in that delivery.

Include

Mobile banking

MFSYS · Mobile Lead · Bank release · 2018 — 2024

For customers of a bank in Syria, on their own phone.

A person signs in, sends money, saves a beneficiary, pays a bill, checks a loan, and reaches support. The same app shipped as two bank flavors, BEMO and FMFI. Login is tied to the device, and fingerprint can unlock it. It is not on the public Play Store because the bank distributes it.

My part

I delivered the Flutter app with the MFSYS team and the bank, including the on-site go-live in Syria. Push notifications and the separate bank builds were part of that release.

Cargo Guide

Truck tracking

MFSYS · Mobile Lead · Play Store

For a driver on the road, and for the broker watching that load.

The driver books cargo and marks delivery status. The broker sees live GPS on a map and can message the driver. Status changes also arrive as a push notification, so nobody has to keep the screen open to know the truck moved.

My part

Flutter maps, live GPS, booking, chat, and notifications, released on the Play Store with the MFSYS team.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • REST

CustomerCore

Small-business books

MFSYS · Mobile Lead · Play Store · English and German

For a small business owner who already uses CustomerCore on the web.

The phone app shows profit, revenue, expenses, and cashflow for a date range. The owner keeps contacts, writes quotations, and sets reminders. A photo of a receipt can fill the title, amount, and line items. Sign-in can use a fingerprint, and alerts arrive by push. It is the companion to the Swiss web product.

My part

Flutter with GetX for screens, Dio for the API, and Hive for data kept on the phone. Biometrics, push, and the receipt scan shipped with the team to the Play Store.

  • Flutter
  • GetX
  • Dio
  • Hive
  • REST
  • Biometrics
  • FCM
  • Play Store

CiiHive Paperless

Bank staff app

MFSYS · Mobile Lead · Kotlin · Not a public store app

For bank staff at a branch, not for the customer.

Staff fill forms even when the network is down, then sync later. The app also checks identity with biometrics. This one is native Android, not Flutter, because it sits next to the bank’s existing Android tools.

My part

Kotlin and Java on the Android SDK: the offline forms and the biometric check, with the MFSYS team.
